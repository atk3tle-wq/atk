Programmes

Two pathways into Singapore Sign Language — pick the one that fits how you want to learn.

Term Classes

Structured, Progressive — Level 1 to 3

4 terms a year. Each term is 10 lessons plus an assessment, one evening session a week (with a second weekly session possible if demand and classroom space allow). Finish with a Certificate of Completion; Grade A students may advance a level.

$330 / term 8–10 students Face-to-face

Standalone Classes

Beginner Classes — Try SgSL First

A self-contained 5-session taster, completely separate from the term pathway. Flexible start dates, with new 5-week cycles beginning continuously, staggered every 2–3 weeks. Ends with a Certificate of Attendance.

$250 Face-to-face or Zoom
