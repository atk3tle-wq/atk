Programme Description

Level 2 deepens your understanding of SgSL nuance and Deaf culture. You'll build confidence in everyday conversation and strengthen expressive and receptive skills through storytelling and narration.

Learning Outcomes

Through direct immersion, students will:

  • Extend fingerspelling and vocabulary into more complex conversation
  • Use classifiers, eye gaze, and non-manual markers to shape meaning
  • Narrate short stories and everyday events in SgSL
  • Deepen understanding of Deaf culture and community norms
  • Hold two-way conversations with increasing spontaneity

Format & Platform

  • 10 lessons + assessment per term, one evening session a week (with an optional second weekly session, depending on demand and classroom availability)
  • Class size: 8–10 students, face-to-face
  • Resources: full access to SgSLcore (our complete sign-video library) plus compiled notes with linked videos
  • Homework: not assigned
  • Certificate: Certificate of Completion for all who finish; distinctions for high performers, with Grade A eligible to advance a level (exact distinction threshold to be confirmed)

Who It's For

Learners who have completed Level 1 with SgSL Space or Equal Dreams, or who can demonstrate equivalent foundational competency.

Prerequisite: Completion of Level 1 (SgSL Space or Equal Dreams), normally within the last 6 months. See the progression FAQ if it's been longer.

Pricing

$330 per term (10 lessons + assessment). Payment via PayNow Corporate or Stripe (card/invoicing). Your seat is confirmed upon payment, first come first served. No refunds once paid, unless the term is cancelled.

Registration

Registration opens ahead of the January 2027 launch intake. Email us to be notified the moment sign-ups open.
