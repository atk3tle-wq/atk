Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about classes, pricing, and the move from Equal Dreams.

General

How do I register for a class?
Head to the Programmes page and choose Level 1–3 or a Standalone class, then email us or use the registration link once it opens. If registration hasn't opened yet, email hello@sgslspace.sg to be notified first.
How do I pay, and when is my seat confirmed?
We accept PayNow Corporate and Stripe (card or invoicing). Your seat is only confirmed once payment is received, on a first-come, first-served basis.
Can I get a refund if I can't attend anymore?
There are no refunds once payment is made, unless SgSL Space cancels the class or term.
Is there homework?
No — SgSL Space does not assign homework. This is a deliberate change from how classes were run previously.
How many students are in each class?
Term classes and Standalone Face-to-Face classes run at 8–10 students; Standalone Zoom classes run at 6–9. If a class doesn't reach the minimum size, we'll contact you before it starts.
What if I have accessibility needs?
Let us know in your registration, and we'll do our best to accommodate you — including interpretation or materials in accessible formats where needed.
Where are classes held?
We're securing a dedicated venue (at least 50 sqm, two classrooms) ahead of the January 2027 launch. We'll publish the address once the lease is confirmed.
Can I catch up if I miss a class?
Zoom recordings are available for catch-up, limited to 2 per student, in line with attendance requirements for the term assessment.

Term Classes (Level 1–3)

How many terms run a year?
Four — Term 1 (Jan–Mar), Term 2 (Apr–Jun), Term 3 (Jul–Sep), Term 4 (Oct–Dec). See the full 2027 Schedule.
How am I assessed?
Each term ends with an assessment after 10 lessons. The exact format — practical, written, or both — is still being finalised and will be published before Term 1 registration opens.
How does the distinction / Grade A system work?
All who finish receive a Certificate of Completion; strong performers receive a distinction. The exact scoring threshold for a distinction or Grade A is still being confirmed. Grade A students are eligible to advance directly to the next level.
Can I skip straight to Level 2 or 3?
Only if you've completed the prior level — with SgSL Space or with Equal Dreams — normally within the last 6 months, or achieved a Grade A distinction. If more time has passed, email us and we'll advise on the best pathway.
What's SgSLcore, and do I get access?
SgSLcore is our sign-video resource library. Term class students get full access to every sign taught across all levels; Standalone students get access only to the videos from their own sessions.

Standalone Classes

How is a Standalone class different from Level 1?
A Standalone class is a self-contained 5-session taster with a Certificate of Attendance — it doesn't lead into Level 2. Level 1 is the first step of the full, progressive Level 1–3 pathway.
When can I start a Standalone class?
Anytime — new 5-week cycles begin continuously, staggered every 2–3 weeks, face-to-face or on Zoom.
If I enjoy my Standalone class, can I move into the term pathway?
Yes, though it's a separate registration — completing a Standalone class doesn't automatically enrol you in Level 1.

Transitioning from Equal Dreams

Why are SgSL classes moving to SgSL Space?
From the January 2027 intake, SgSL classes move from Equal Dreams to SgSL Space — a new, dedicated organisation founded and owned by longtime SgSL specialists Andrew Tay and Yew Hau En, focused solely on SgSL education and community development.
Will my instructor stay the same?
In most cases, yes. Many instructors who taught SgSL at Equal Dreams continue teaching at SgSL Space.
I completed Level 1 with Equal Dreams. Can I go straight into Level 2?
Yes. Completion of Level 1 with Equal Dreams is recognised for direct entry into Level 2 at SgSL Space, subject to the usual 6-month progression window.
I completed Level 2 with Equal Dreams. Can I go straight into Level 3?
Yes, on the same basis as above.
I completed Level 3 with Equal Dreams. What's next?
SgSL Space is planning a broader range of advanced courses beyond the original Level 3 curriculum. We'll announce these as they're finalised.
What happens to my student records?
Relevant learning records and course progress are being transferred from Equal Dreams to SgSL Space to ensure continuity. This handover agreement is being finalised ahead of launch.
Will course fees change?
Yes — SgSL Space's own pricing is listed on each programme page. It differs from Equal Dreams' previous sliding-scale model.
How can I stay updated?
Follow SgSL Space's social channels (linked in the footer) or email hello@sgslspace.sg to be added to our list. Equal Dreams will also share updates on their own channels until launch.

Still have a question?

We're happy to help — reach out directly and we'll get back to you.
