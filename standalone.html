Programme Description

Standalone classes are a self-contained taste of Singapore Sign Language, completely separate from the Level 1–3 term pathway. Completing a Standalone class does not grant entry into Level 2 — it's designed purely to help you explore SgSL and Deaf culture with no long-term commitment.

Format

TypeSessionsSizePriceFormat

  • Flexible start — new 5-week cycles begin continuously, staggered every 2–3 weeks
  • Resources: notes with videos of signs taught in class only (no full SgSLcore access)
  • Certificate: Certificate of Attendance

Who It's For

Anyone curious about SgSL who wants a low-commitment way to try it — no prior experience needed. If you think you might want to continue on to Level 1 afterwards, that's a separate registration; a Standalone class doesn't carry over into the term pathway.

Pricing

$250 for the 5-session cycle, face-to-face or Zoom. Payment via PayNow Corporate or Stripe. Seats confirmed upon payment, first come first served. No refunds once paid, unless the class is cancelled.

Registration

New cycles open on a rolling basis. Email us to find out the next available start date.
